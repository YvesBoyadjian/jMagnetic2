Documentation from the original game packages

Magnetic Scrolls included various goodies and manuals with their game distributions. Despite the atmospheric value for the games, they often contain important clues for solving the games. Additionally they are required to pass the copy protection Magnetic Scrolls added to their games. After 100 steps you will be prompted for a specific word from the game manuals otherwise you can not continue the game. The extraction programs for the story files will offer to remove this protection. A growing amount of scanned material from the original packages can be found at the Magnetic Scrolls Memorial web site.
